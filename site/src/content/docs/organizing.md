## Folders

Folders can hold notes, boards and other folders. Notes outside any folder are listed under **Unfiled**.

- Drag notes, boards and folders in the sidebar to reorder them or move them into a folder. **Move to** in an item's menu does the same.
- Select several items with `Ctrl`-click (or `Shift`-click for a range) and move or delete them together.
- A folder's page shows its contents as cards. Pick how much each card shows (*Full*, *Cover* or *Title*), sort the notes, or filter them by text.
- New notes go into the folder you're in.

## Favorites, pins and recent notes

- The star at the top of a note adds it to **Favorites** on the overview.
- **Pin to top** in a note's menu keeps it at the top of its list.
- **Recent** in the sidebar lists the notes you edited last, and the overview offers to continue where you left off.

## Search and commands

`Ctrl` + `K` (or **Search** at the top of the sidebar) opens the command palette. It finds notes by title and text, with the match highlighted, as well as folders, boards and tags. It also runs commands: a new note, folder or board, today's journal, the theme, a backup and more.

## Today's journal

One note per day: **Today's journal** on the overview, in the command palette, or `Ctrl` + `Alt` + `J`. It's made from the journal template the first time each day, and opens the same note again after that. Journal notes go into a *Journal* folder, or wherever you moved the last one.

## Quick capture

Type a thought into **Write something down…** at the top of the overview and press `Enter`: it's kept as a new note in *Unfiled*, and the field is ready for the next one. `Shift` + `Enter` starts a new line; the first line becomes the title.

## The Trash and Activity

Deleting moves a note, folder or board to the Trash, and the message that follows offers **Undo**. **Activity** (in the sidebar) has the Trash under *Deleted*: things wait there for 30 days and can be restored, together with their cards on boards.

Activity also lists what changed and every message Lodoc showed you, so a message that closed by itself is never lost.
