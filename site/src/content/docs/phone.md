Lodoc works in your phone's browser, and even better as an installed app: it opens like any other app, works offline, and can receive things you share from other apps.

## Install Lodoc

- **Android (Chrome):** open [lodoc.app](https://lodoc.app/), then the browser menu (`⋮`), and **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**.
- **iPhone and iPad (Safari):** the Share button, then **Add to Home Screen**.
- **Computer (Chrome, Edge):** the install icon at the end of the address bar.

On Android and on computers, the installed app uses the same notes as the browser it was installed from. On iPhone and iPad, the Home Screen app keeps its own notes, separate from Safari: move them over with a backup.

## Share to Lodoc

In the installed app on Android, Lodoc appears in the share sheet of other apps. Share text, a link or images, and they land at the top of one note, *Collected*, each as a dated line: "20.10.2026: …". Lodoc opens that note so you can see it arrive.

To collect somewhere else, choose **Collect here** in any note's menu.

## Writing on a phone

- The format bar above the keyboard has undo and redo, the block type, lists, checklists, indenting and text styles.
- Swipe from the left edge to open the sidebar, and tap outside it to close it.
- Press and hold a note, folder or board in the sidebar for its menu.
- On Android, press and hold the Lodoc icon on your home screen for **Today's journal**.

Sharing to Lodoc works in the installed app on Android. iPhones don't let web apps receive shares.
