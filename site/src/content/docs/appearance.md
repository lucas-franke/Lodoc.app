**Appearance** at the bottom of the sidebar changes how Lodoc looks. Every change shows right away, with a small preview at the top. **Randomize** picks a look for you, and **Reset all** goes back to the start.

## Theme

- **Light, dark or system**: also the sun and moon at the top right of every page.
- **Tint**: Sand, Rose, Sky, Sage or Lavender, soft in light mode and in tinted darks in dark mode. *Neutral* keeps it grey.
- **Gradient**: a gentle diagonal gradient behind the page.
- **Text colour**: neutral, matching the tint, or a tint of its own.

## Pages

- **Font**: sans serif or serif. Both come with Lodoc, so nothing is loaded from the internet.
- **Text size**: small, medium or large.
- **Automatic covers**: turn off to show only the covers you picked.
- **Texture**: *Paper*, *Laid paper* or a *Dot grid* on the page, with its strength.

## Interface

- **Sidebar**: connected, or floating with a little space around it, on wide screens.
- **Corners**: sharp, rounded or round.
- **Grain**: a fine grain on the sidebar, menus and panels.

Your look is saved in this browser and included in backups. Merging a backup keeps your own look, and only *Replace* takes over the one in the file.
