A board is a canvas you can pan and zoom, to lay out a topic: your notes as cards, text, images, and arrows between them. Its name sits large at the top left, like a note's title: click it to rename the board. A new board starts there, ready for its name. Like notes, boards can be added to your favorites (the star at the top right), pinned to the top of their list, or duplicated, from their menu.

## Adding things

- The round **+** button adds a note, some text, images or a frame.
- Drag a note from the sidebar onto the board to place it right there.
- Paste an image with `Ctrl` + `V`, or drop image files onto the board.

Cards show your notes as they are: editing a note anywhere updates every board it's on. Double-click a card (or use its pencil) to edit the note right on the board. Removing a card never deletes the note.

## Arrows and frames

- Drag from one of the dots on an item's sides to another item to draw an arrow, or use **Connect to…** in its menu. Select an arrow to label it, reverse it or delete it.
- A **frame** groups what lies on it, like "To do" or "Ideas". With items selected, a new frame is drawn around them. Moving a frame moves everything on it.

## Keeping it tidy

- **Tidy up** (the grid button at the top right) lines everything up in neat rows without overlaps. Frames keep their cards, and `Ctrl` + `Z` puts everything back.
- **Cards:** at the top right sets how much every note card shows: *Full*, *Cover* or *Title*. Each card can also be set on its own in its menu.
- **Find on board** (the magnifier at the top right) searches the cards, text and frames on the board and jumps to the match.
- The zoom bar at the bottom has zoom in and out, the current zoom (click it for 100 %), *fit all*, and a minimap.

Boards can live in folders, next to your notes.
