Lodoc is a place for notes and boards that lives in your browser. There is no account to make and nothing to set up: open [lodoc.app](https://lodoc.app/) and start writing. Your notes stay on your device, and Lodoc keeps working without an internet connection once it has been opened.

## Your first note

1. Click **Start writing** on the welcome screen, or press `Ctrl` + `Alt` + `N` anywhere.
2. Type a title, then press `Enter` to go to the text.
3. Write. Type `/` at the start of a line for headings, lists, checklists, tables, images and more.

There is no Save button: everything is kept as you type. A new note that you leave without writing anything isn't kept, so trying things out never leaves empty notes behind.

## Find your way around

- **The sidebar** holds your folders, boards and notes. `Ctrl` + `\` shows or hides it.
- **The overview** (at the top of the sidebar) shows what you edited last, your favorites, and a field to jot down a quick thought.
- **Search** at the top of the sidebar, or `Ctrl` + `K`, finds any note by its title or text, and runs commands.
- **Lodoc ⌄** next to the name opens this documentation, what's new, the getting-started guide and all keyboard shortcuts.

## Take the tour

**Take the tour** on the welcome screen (or *Getting started guide* in the command palette) adds a note that shows the basics by example, with a short checklist to try them. Edit it, tick things off, and delete it when you're done.

## Keep a copy

Because your notes live in this browser, clearing the browser's data would remove them. Export a backup now and then from **Backup** at the bottom of the sidebar. [Backups and moving devices](../backup/) explains your options, including automatic backups.
