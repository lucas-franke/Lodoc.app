Lodoc has no server for your notes. Everything you write, every image and every setting is stored in your browser, on your device, and nothing is sent anywhere.

## What Lodoc doesn't do

- No account, and no sign-in.
- No analytics, tracking or ads.
- No fonts or scripts from other sites: everything Lodoc needs comes with it.

When you open lodoc.app, your browser loads the app from its host (Cloudflare, or GitHub Pages for the GitHub address), like any website. Hosts usually keep short technical logs of such visits. Your notes are never part of that.

## Where your data is

- **Notes, folders, boards and images:** in the browser's storage for lodoc.app (IndexedDB).
- **Settings** like your look, sidebar width and the backup reminder: in the browser's local storage.
- **Note history and Activity:** on this device only, and not part of backups.

Each browser and each device has its own notes. On Android and on computers, the installed app shares them with the browser it was installed from; on iPhone and iPad, the Home Screen app has its own. The two addresses, lodoc.app and the GitHub one, are separate sites to the browser, so they don't share notes either.

## Your files

Backups, downloads and automatic backups go only where you put them. When you send a backup with **Send backup…**, it goes wherever you choose in the share sheet.

## Removing everything

Delete things in Lodoc and empty the Trash (*Activity → Deleted*), or clear the site data for lodoc.app in your browser's settings. That removes everything Lodoc stored on this device, so export a backup first if you want to keep it.
