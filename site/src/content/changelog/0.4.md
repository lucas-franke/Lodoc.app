## New

- **Today's journal**: one note per day, from the overview, the command palette, `Ctrl+Alt+J`, or the app icon on Android (press and hold).
- **Note history**: Lodoc keeps earlier versions of a note on this device. Open **History…** in a note's menu to look at one or put it back.
- **Share to Lodoc** from other apps on your phone: text, links and images land at the top of one note, "Collected", each with the date. Choose **Collect here** in any note's menu to collect somewhere else.
- **Import Markdown**: `.md` files, a whole folder (an Obsidian vault) or a `.zip` (a Notion export) become notes and folders, with their images, tags and links between them.
- **#tags in the text**: write `#idea` anywhere in a note and it gets the tag. Typing `#` suggests the tags you already use. `Ctrl`-click a tag to see all its notes.
- **Move notes between phone and computer**: **Send backup…** passes your notes to the share sheet. Share a backup file to Lodoc on the other device, and choose **Merge**.
- **Quick capture**: type a thought at the top of the overview, and Enter keeps it as a new note.
- **Your own templates**: choose **Use as template** in any note's menu.
- **Tidy up** a board: everything in neat rows without overlaps, frames keep their cards, and Undo is one click away.
- **A welcome** on first start, with three ways in: start writing, take the tour, or restore a backup.
- Two new page textures, **Laid paper** and **Dot grid**, a new **Paper** texture, and **Randomize** for a surprise look.
- Typed arrows become arrows: `->` → , `<-` ← , `<->` ↔ , `=>` ⇒ .
- The search field in the sidebar opens the command palette.
- **About Lodoc**: a short page on what Lodoc is, for anyone you'd like to show it to. Try the looks right on it, or press Randomize.
- **Docs**: how Lodoc works, page by page, from getting started to backups and your data. Open them from **Lodoc ⌄** at the top of the sidebar.
- **What's new**: this page, with every week's changes (and an RSS feed). Open it from the new **Lodoc ⌄** menu at the top of the sidebar, which also has the guide, the shortcuts and the version you are on.
- Small joys: a ticked box pops and a finished list sends up a few dots, the overview says good morning, seven quiet dots show the days you wrote, Shuffle on a cover, small drawings for empty places, and board cards that settle where they land.

## Improved

- On phones, the format bar puts undo, lists and indenting first.
- Bigger buttons in the sidebar on touch screens, and the command palette opens above the keyboard.
- Messages appear in the corner on wide screens, out of the way of your text.
- One date format everywhere: 20.10.2026.
- Empty lines in templates say what goes there.
- A new board starts with its name field ready, and previews keep the lines of a note apart.
- The backup menu shows when the browser keeps your notes safe.
- Merging a backup no longer brings back what you deleted since, and keeps your own look.

## Fixed

- The first empty line of a note can be deleted.
- No white strip under the app on phones.
- Empty notes left by a reload no longer pile up.
- Long titles wrap instead of being cut off, also on phones.
- Items added to a board no longer land on top of each other.
- Table lines follow the theme, and shortcuts don't fire while a menu is open.
- Better structure for screen readers.
