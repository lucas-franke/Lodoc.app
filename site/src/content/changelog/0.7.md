## Improved

- **Importing a backup is safer.** The dialog shows what is in the file and what is in Lodoc now. **Merge** is the main button. **Replace all…** asks once more and names what will be deleted, and the message afterwards offers **Undo**, which brings everything back until you close or reload Lodoc.
- Restoring a backup into an empty Lodoc no longer asks how: there is nothing to merge with.
- One word for the file: **Export backup** and **Import backup…** replace *Export all notes* and *Import from file…*.

## Fixed

- After a backup replaced everything, the Scratchpad on the overview could stay empty until a reload.
