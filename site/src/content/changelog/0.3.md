## New

- **Boards**: an endless canvas for a topic, with note cards, text, images, arrows between them, and frames to group them. Cards show your notes as they are, and you can edit a note right on its card.
- **Tags** under a note's title, a page for every tag, and **Links between notes**: type `@` to link to another note. Each note lists the notes that link to it.
- **Templates** for meeting notes, a daily journal, a project plan and a to-do list.
- **Install Lodoc as an app.** It keeps working without a connection once it has been opened.
- **Appearance**: a soft colour tint, a gradient, page font, text size, a floating sidebar, corner roundness, a paper grain and the text colour, all with a live preview.
- **Trash**: deleted things wait 30 days and can be restored, with their cards on boards.
- **Activity**: what changed, what was deleted and every message the app showed you.
- Download a note as Markdown, or a folder as a `.zip` of Markdown files.
- Select several items in the sidebar with `Ctrl`-click or `Shift`-click, and move or delete them together.
- A list of all keyboard shortcuts: press `?`.

## Improved

- Images are made smaller as you add them, so notes stay light.
- Copying text no longer copies whole images into the plain text.
- Quotes and the hint in empty lines are easier to read.
