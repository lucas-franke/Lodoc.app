## New

- Lodoc is online at [lodoc.app](https://lodoc.app/). The app was called LocalNotes before; your notes stayed where they were.
- **Gradient covers**: every note gets a colour gradient of its own, until you pick another one or an image.
- **Duplicate** a note, or a folder with everything in it.
- A Lodoc icon for the browser tab.

## Fixed

- The sidebar keeps its width.
