## New

- **Settings** at the bottom of the sidebar (or `Ctrl` + `,`), with a tab each for Appearance, Accessibility and Backup.
- **High contrast** in Settings → Accessibility: darker text, clearer lines and bold focus outlines.
- **Reduce motion** in Settings → Accessibility: no animations, things change at once. *Full* keeps the motion even if your system asks for less.
- Contrast and motion follow your system at first, and can be switched from the command palette too.
- Settings → Backup shows how much space Lodoc uses in this browser.

## Improved

- A quieter page header: just the star and ⋯ (downloads are in ⋯), and light and dark live in Settings → Appearance.
- The Appearance panel is a little wider, and the page beside it is the preview (on a phone, a small note at the top).
- The Backup menu is shorter: export and imports, with automatic backups and keeping data safe one click away in **Backup settings…**.
- Chosen options in Appearance are easier to see, with a check and a strong outline, and every control has an outline you can make out.
- Text colour is now a single **Tinted text** switch.
- The search field at the top of the sidebar stands out from the rows below it.
- The search field in the command palette and the board pickers has a clear outline.
