## New

- **High contrast** in Appearance → Accessibility: darker text, clearer lines and bold focus outlines. It follows your system at first.
- **Reduce motion** in Appearance → Accessibility: no animations, things change at once. It follows your system at first, and *Full* keeps the motion even if your system asks for less.

## Improved

- Appearance opens in a window in the middle, with its sections on the left (tabs on a phone), from the new palette button at the top right of every page. Light and dark are in there too.
- Text colour is now a single **Tinted text** switch.
