## New

- **Accessibility menu** at the top right of every page, next to Appearance:
  - **High contrast**: darker text, clearer lines and bold focus outlines.
  - **Reduce motion**: no animations, things change at once. *Full* keeps the motion even if your system asks for less.
  - Both follow your system at first, and can be switched from the command palette too.

## Improved

- Appearance opens from the palette button at the top right of every page, where the sun and moon used to be. Light and dark are in there, the panel is a little wider, and the page beside it is the preview (on a phone, a small note at the top).
- Chosen options in Appearance are easier to see, with a check and a strong outline, and every control has an outline you can make out.
- Text colour is now a single **Tinted text** switch.
- The search field at the top of the sidebar stands out from the rows below it.
- The search field in the command palette and the board pickers has a clear outline.
