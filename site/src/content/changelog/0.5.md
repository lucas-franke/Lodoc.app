## New

- **Settings** at the bottom of the sidebar (or `Ctrl` + `,`), with a section each for Appearance, Accessibility and Backup.
- **High contrast** in Settings → Accessibility: darker text, clearer lines and bold focus outlines.
- **Reduce motion** in Settings → Accessibility: no animations, things change at once. *Full* keeps the motion even if your system asks for less.
- Contrast and motion follow your system at first, and can be switched from the command palette too.
- Settings → Backup shows how much space Lodoc uses in this browser.

## Improved

- A quieter page header: just the star and ⋯ (downloads are in ⋯), and light and dark live in Settings → Appearance.
- Appearance has a bigger preview, with the sidebar and a note, that stays in view while you scroll its settings.
- The Backup menu is shorter: export and imports, with automatic backups and keeping data safe one click away in **Backup settings…**.
- Chosen options in Settings are filled in the text colour (black, or white in dark mode) with a check, and every control has an outline you can make out.
- Text colour is now a single **Tinted text** switch.
- A board shows its name large at the top left, like a note's title, and a new board starts there, ready to be named.
- Find on board is a small button at the top right of a board; the search field opens when you need it.
- Boards can be favorited, pinned to the top and duplicated, like notes.
- The Backup button shows its status in a small label next to it: grey while all is well, amber with a filled alert icon once a backup is due.
- Settings opens right away, also the first time.
- Icons are a little larger, so they are easier to see and to hit; the ones in long lists stay small and calm.
- The search field at the top of the sidebar stands out from the rows below it.
- The search field in the command palette and the board pickers has a clear outline.
- Tidy up on a board works quietly, without a message or an Activity entry; Ctrl + Z puts everything back.
- A new Lodoc logo and app icon, with a bolder outline that is easier to make out at small sizes.
- Settings → Backup shows the chosen backup folder clearly, with its actions right beside it, and Keep data safe simply says **Kept safe** once it's on.
- Settings is now a page of its own, like Activity, without the preview: every change shows right away in the app around it. `Esc` goes back to where you were.
- Settings and Activity have a **Back to …** link above their title that names where you came from; `Esc` does the same on both.
- Page textures lie only on the background now: covers, cards, buttons and fields stay plain, so the texture is calmer.
- The shortcut list shows the checklist shortcut as two keys, [ and ], so the brackets no longer look like a box.
- Keyboard focus is easy to see everywhere, on the website too: a clear 2px outline in a deep grey that follows your tint.
- In menus and the command palette, the highlighted item has a small bar at its left edge, so you can see where the arrow keys are.
- Settings, Activity, tags and the overview have no empty bar at the top any more (it stays while the sidebar is hidden, for its button), and no line under their tabs.
- On a phone, the sidebar closes once you pick Activity, Settings or a tag, like it does for notes.
- On the website, the appearance demo shows four ready-made looks: three light ones and a dark one in high contrast, picked by their swatches.
- The website's links are blue and underlined, and its outlined buttons have a clearly visible edge.
