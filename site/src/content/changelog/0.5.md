## New

- **Settings** at the bottom of the sidebar (or `Ctrl` + `,`), with a section each for Appearance, Accessibility and Backup.
- **High contrast** in Settings → Accessibility: darker text, clearer lines and bold focus outlines.
- **Reduce motion** in Settings → Accessibility: no animations, things change at once. *Full* keeps the motion even if your system asks for less.
- Contrast and motion follow your system at first, and can be switched from the command palette too.
- Settings → Backup shows how much space Lodoc uses in this browser.
- **Pinned on the overview**: the box at the top of the overview is now a note of its own for what you want to keep in view, with bold, lists and checklists. It replaces *Write something down*, which only made new notes (New note does that).
- **Polaroids** on boards: an image can be shown in a white frame, slightly tilted, with a caption under the picture (the buttons in its toolbar). The caption also describes the image for screen readers and Find on board.

## Improved

- A quieter page header: just the star and ⋯ (downloads are in ⋯), and light and dark live in Settings → Appearance.
- The Backup menu is shorter: export and imports, with automatic backups and keeping data safe one click away in **Backup settings…**.
- Chosen options in Settings are filled in the text colour (black, or white in dark mode) with a check, and every control has an outline you can make out.
- Text colour is now a single **Tinted text** switch.
- A board shows its name large at the top left, like a note's title, and a new board starts there, ready to be named.
- Find on board is a small button at the top right of a board; the search field opens when you need it.
- Boards can be favorited, pinned to the top and duplicated, like notes.
- The Backup button shows its status in a small label next to it: grey while all is well, amber with a filled alert icon once a backup is due.
- Settings opens right away, also the first time.
- Icons are a little larger, so they are easier to see and to hit; the ones in long lists stay small and calm.
- The search field at the top of the sidebar stands out from the rows below it.
- The search field in the command palette and the board pickers has a clear outline.
- Tidy up on a board works quietly, without a message or an Activity entry; Ctrl + Z puts everything back.
- A new Lodoc logo and app icon, with a bolder outline that is easier to make out at small sizes.
- Settings → Backup shows the chosen backup folder clearly, with its actions right beside it, and Keep data safe simply says **Kept safe** once it's on.
- Settings is now a page of its own, like Activity, without the preview: every change shows right away in the app around it. `Esc` goes back to where you were.
- Settings and Activity have a **Back to …** link above their title that names where you came from; `Esc` does the same on both.
- Page textures lie only on the background now: covers, cards, buttons and fields stay plain, so the texture is calmer.
- The shortcut list shows the checklist shortcut as two keys, [ and ], so the brackets no longer look like a box.
- Keyboard focus is easy to see everywhere, on the website too: a clear 2px outline in a deep grey that follows your tint.
- In menus and the command palette, the highlighted item has a small bar at its left edge, so you can see where the arrow keys are.
- Settings, Activity, tags and the overview have no empty bar at the top any more (it stays while the sidebar is hidden, for its button), and no line under their tabs.
- Less text to read: Settings, Activity, the shortcut list and a few menus drop explanations you don't need, and options show up only when they apply (Tinted text once you pick a tint; Gradient not while high contrast is on).
- Shortcuts show ⌘ and ⌥ on a Mac.
- The backup status beside the Backup button is just the when ("3 days ago"), so it no longer gets cut off.
- Activity's *Deleted* tab is now called **Trash**, like everywhere else; the overview leaves out counts of zero.
- On a phone, the sidebar closes once you pick Activity, Settings or a tag, like it does for notes.
- On the website, the appearance demo shows four ready-made looks: three light ones and a dark one in high contrast, picked by their swatches.
- The website's links are blue and underlined, and its outlined buttons have a clearly visible edge.
- On a phone, the block type menu in the format bar offers the types you use most (paragraph, three headings, the lists and quote), so it fits above the keyboard.
- On touch screens, a card on a board moves only once you've tapped it, so swiping across cards pans the board instead of rearranging it.
- On touch screens, the corners for resizing board items, the dots for drawing arrows, checklist boxes, tags and the small buttons on cards are easier to hit. The board's zoom bar leaves out its slider there (zoom with two fingers) to make room for bigger buttons.
- Long press a note, folder or board in the sidebar to open its menu, or move your finger to drag it. The browser's link preview and text selection no longer get in the way.
- A phone held sideways gets the phone layout: the sidebar opens over the page and closes once you pick something, and notes have the format bar.
- On a tablet, notes have the format bar at the bottom too, beside the sidebar, with Undo and the block types, instead of a toolbar over the selected text.
- On a phone, a note's cover is lower and its title a little smaller, so the text starts higher up; Shuffle and Remove on the cover are icons there.
- On touch screens, the command palette leaves out keyboard shortcuts.
- The *Getting started* guide names the button first and the shortcut second, so it reads right on a phone too, and points to Settings → Appearance.
- The help page about phones explains moving and resizing cards on a board, and holding a row in the sidebar to move it.
- The grey hints on empty lines, *Empty note* on cards and the hint in an empty folder are upright, like the rest of the interface.
- **New note cards**, on the overview, in folders and tags and on boards: the cover on top, then the title, then the start of the note in its own shape (headings, bullets, numbered steps, checklist items with their ticks), and one line with when it was edited, how much of its checklist is done and its tags.
- Covers are calmer, with darker tones in dark mode, and a gradient a note got by itself is a thin band of colour on its card; covers you picked keep their full size.
- Folder cards list their newest notes, and board cards draw a small map of the board.
- On a phone, cards come in two columns; on large screens the overview, folders and tags use more of the width for cards.
- On a board, a card's pencil and ⋯ appear when you point at it or select it, so the title has the card's full width.
- A calmer Settings page with one look throughout: every choice has its label above and its options as the same small chips, each with a radio dot and a little picture of what it does (a sun or moon, the tint's colour, *Ag* in each font, *Aa* in each size). On and off settings are a switch beside their label.
- The tints in Settings show their names, so you can read them on a phone too.
- **Randomize** and **Reset all appearance settings** sit together at the end of the Appearance tab, each with its Undo. Contrast and motion say what your system asks for right now.
- Fewer buttons on the overview and folder pages: **New note** (and *Today's journal* on the overview) next to one **New…** menu for a folder, a board or a note from a template. The *Continue where you left off* line is slimmer, so the cards start higher up.
- Dates are written out, *20 Oct 2026* and *Thursday, 29 October 2026*, instead of in numbers.
- On touch screens, only the sidebar row of the page you're on shows its buttons, so the list stays calm; press and hold any row for its menu.
- Titles in Serif are semibold and less tightly spaced.- Zoomed out on a board, the names of cards and frames stay readable, like place names on a map, so *fit all* on a phone shows which card is which.
- On a phone, the /, @ and # menus end above the format bar instead of running under it, and the / menu fits more rows above the keyboard: they are shorter, and when there is little room around the cursor the line moves up first.

## Fixed

- **Back to …** on Settings or Activity leads to the overview when the page you came from has been deleted, instead of to an empty page.
- Going from Settings to Activity and back no longer loses the way back to your note.
- On a phone, **Move to** lists the folders right in the menu instead of opening off the edge of the screen.
- On a phone, the format bar no longer slides down over the page each time a note opens.
- Messages sit above the format bar and a board's + button instead of covering them.
- A board's name no longer runs under the buttons beside it on narrow screens; it is cut off with … instead.
- On an iPhone or iPad, the page no longer zooms in when you tap into search, a tag, a name you are changing or Find on board.
- In the command palette, a long folder name no longer hides the note's title.
- With the text size set to Small, an iPhone or iPad no longer zooms in when you tap into a note or a board's text.
- Italic text in Serif notes uses Source Serif's own italic letters instead of slanted upright ones.
- A favorite's star and a pin on a card are filled, so they no longer look like buttons.
- Board cards no longer cut their text off halfway through a line; the last line fades out.
- Checkboxes follow the theme and the tint instead of the browser's blue.
- A message's close button sits inside its top-right corner.
- A board's name has a soft backdrop on every screen, so frames and cards under it don't run into it.
